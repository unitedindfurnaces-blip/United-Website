Equipment Erection & Commissioning

United provides professional turnkey solutions for the erection and commissioning of industrial furnaces and their critical auxiliary equipment. Our approach is anchored by a senior team of design and site engineers who oversee every phase of the installation—from structural framework assembly and refractory lining to the integration of fuel trains and control systems. By deploying a specialized workforce of skilled contractors and site technicians, we ensure that complex components like recuperators, combustion blowers, and automated charging machines are precisely aligned and rigorously tested. This meticulous on-site execution guarantees that your thermal assets transition smoothly from construction to full-load operation with maximum safety and efficiency.

Onsite Execution Scope of Work

  • 1 Structural & Mechanical Erection

    • Precision assembly of furnace shells, doors, moving hearths, and structural supports.

  • 2 Auxiliary Equipment Integration

    • Installation of critical subsystems including heat exchangers, cooling systems, exhaust stacks, and material handling units.

  • 3 Cold & Hot Commissioning

    • Comprehensive testing of mechanical movements, sensor calibration, and initial firing sequences under strict engineering supervision.

  • 4 Site Management

    • Dedicated project leads and contractors ensure compliance with local safety regulations and ISO quality standards throughout the build.
